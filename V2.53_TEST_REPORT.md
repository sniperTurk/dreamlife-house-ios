## Limitations

The comparison checks **raw-byte equality**, not which copy is newer or semantically better. Different JSON encodings of equivalent game states may be labeled different. The UserDefaults triple-read snapshot remains best effort rather than a cross-process atomic transaction. CRC32 is not authentication and recovery archives are unencrypted. No automatic conflict resolution or import is provided.
