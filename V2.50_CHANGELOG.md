# DreamLife House v2.50.0 — build 70

## Preserve equal-sequence primary/pending journal conflicts
- Fixed a reproducible save-data loss scenario in v2.49: if a committed primary and an interrupted pending journal both had the **same positive commit sequence but different bytes**, the loader discarded the pending copy. The two candidates cannot be ordered from sequence alone.
- The loader now enters a **read-only recovery state** without deleting or rewriting either copy. The existing parent-facing Settings preview shows primary, backup and pending candidates; the local-only recovery archive retains their exact bytes with CRC32 integrity checks.
- Active sessions now check for this same conflict immediately before saving, preventing an externally introduced pending candidate from being overwritten.
- Older pending journals and byte-identical pending journals continue through normal recovery; older backups do not resolve an equal-sequence tie.
- Added five XCTest source cases, one XCUITest source case with an isolated DEBUG-only fixture, and a 22-assertion executable Linux smoke suite. Updated two earlier test suites and two older XCTest expectations to reflect the safer policy.
