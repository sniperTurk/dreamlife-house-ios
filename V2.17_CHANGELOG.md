# DreamLife House v2.17

- Added the original **Dream House Trophy** as a permanent Living Room decoration after completing and claiming the five-badge collection reward.
- The trophy is interactive: admiring it grants +4 Fun (clamped to 100) and records a persistent visit count.
- Trophy interaction is intentionally restricted to the Living Room and remains locked until the collection reward is claimed.
- Added 3 regression tests for unlock gating, room validation/Fun reward, and persistence.
- Version bumped to 2.17.0 (build 37).
