# DreamLife House v2.36.0 — build 56

## Save-game integrity (high priority)
- Fixed a downgrade data-loss path missed by v2.35: a compatible primary save could rotate over a **newer-schema backup** on the next reward or interaction. Newer backups are now preserved byte-for-byte while the compatible primary remains playable and writable.
- When the primary is missing or corrupt and the only backup belongs to a newer build, the game enters a read-only preview state. The existing primary and future backup remain untouched, including after reset, onboarding, or reward attempts.
- Normal compatible backup rotation and recovery behavior remain unchanged.

## Accessibility

- Expanded the static House view wiring contract to cover these six actions.
